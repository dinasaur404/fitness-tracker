PRIVACY POLICY

DATA WE COLLECT

Gains Goblin collects the following data to provide our fitness tracking services:

  • Account information (email, name)
  • Fitness data from connected services (Whoop, Wyze)
  • Food and nutrition logs
  • Progress photos (stored securely)
  • Habit tracking data

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To provide personalized fitness tracking
  • To analyze food photos using AI
  • To track your progress over time
  • To sync with connected fitness devices

DATA STORAGE

Your data is stored securely on Cloudflare's infrastructure:

  • Database: Cloudflare D1
  • Photos: Cloudflare R2 (encrypted)
  • Sessions: Cloudflare KV

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We integrate with:

  • Whoop - For workout and recovery data
  • Wyze - For weight/body composition data
  • Cloudflare Workers AI - For food analysis

YOUR RIGHTS

You can request to:

  • Export your data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Disconnect third-party services
